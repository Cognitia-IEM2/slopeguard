Slope Stability

Physics-based infinite-slope stability screening.

Stability Analysis

SlopeGuard estimates the Factor of Safety using slope angle, soil cohesion, friction angle, unit weight, soil depth and rainfall- induced pore pressure.


A lower Factor of Safety indicates reduced slope stability. Rainfall increases pore-water pressure, reducing effective normal stress and potentially lowering the Factor of Safety.

About SlopeGuard

Physics-based highway landslide early warning system.

What is SlopeGuard?

SlopeGuard is a highway slope monitoring and early warning system designed to identify potentially unstable slope segments using physics-based stability calculations and live rainfall information.

Event → Propagation → Impact → Risk

The system combines terrain, geotechnical properties, rainfall and pore-pressure effects to estimate slope stability and prioritize potentially hazardous highway segments.

Important Limitation

The current prototype uses modeled terrain and soil parameters for demonstration. Real nationwide risk assessment would require validated DEM, soil, geological, road-network and historical landslide datasets.
